# elicitr: Processing and Aggregation of Expert Elicitation Data

Provides tools to import, validate, visualise and aggregate expert
estimates for continuous and categorical variables. Analysis of
continuous variables supports one-, three- and four-point,
confidence-based rescaling and sampling from PERT. Analysis of
categorical variables supports Dirichlet distributions, and weighted
aggregation. You provide the data, and elicitr transforms them into
easily readable graphics.Implements workflows adapted from the IDEA
protocol described by Hemming et al. (2018)
[doi:10.1111/2041-210X.12857](https://doi.org/10.1111/2041-210X.12857)
and categorical elicitation described by Vernet et al. (2024)
[doi:10.1007/s10530-024-03341-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10530-024-03341-2)
.
